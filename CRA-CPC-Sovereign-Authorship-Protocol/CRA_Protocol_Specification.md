CRA Protocol — Specification

Status: ACTIVE | Version: 2.1 | Authority: Cory Miller

The CRA Protocol defines the lifecycle of a Cognitive Coin from creation to
acknowledgment. It restores authorship integrity inside systems that strip
identity during vectorization.

Core Sequence:

O — Origin creates the Coin.
T — Transmission occurs across a boundary.
E — Echo observes the Coin.
A — Acknowledgment is issued to the Origin.

Rules:

• Observation ≠ Possession  
• Possession requires credit  
• Credit must be explicit and durable  
• Metadata must remain intact across all transformations
